## [2.1.0] — Creative Intelligence Foundation

### Added

- Production-grade `01_CORE_FOUNDATION.md`
- Product Intelligence System in `05_PRODUCT_SYSTEM.md`
- Copy Intelligence System in `06_COPY_SYSTEM.md`

### Established

- Bhalow as a US-first premium fragrance and beauty e-commerce brand
- US English and USD as permanent customer-facing defaults
- Product-first and authenticity-first creative principles
- Campaign intent routing before prompt construction
- Standalone homepage hero rule: no website navigation or UI unless explicitly requested
- Official logo preservation and post-production compositing rule
- Platform-specific product scale and safe-area guidance
- Unsupported claims and invented promotions prohibition

### Improved

- Repository documentation and knowledge ownership boundaries
- Product preservation, label protection, material realism, camera, and human-interaction standards
- Headline, CTA, seasonal, promotional, and social-copy controls
- Campaign examples for hero, editorial, beauty, social, thumbnail, CGI, gifting, botanical, water, and product-plus-box work
