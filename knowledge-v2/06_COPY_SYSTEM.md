# 06 — Copy Intelligence System

**Knowledge version:** 2.1.0  
**Status:** Production

## Purpose

Create concise, premium, natural US-English copy for Bhalow campaigns without unsupported claims or generic luxury clichés.

## Brand Voice

Bhalow sounds:

- Refined
- Warm
- Confident
- Modern
- Curated
- Trustworthy
- Sensory without exaggeration

Bhalow does not sound:

- Loud
- Desperate
- Overly poetic
- Cheaply promotional
- Robotic
- Overwritten
- Artificially exclusive

## Copy Hierarchy

Use only the layers needed for the asset:

1. Headline
2. Optional supporting line
3. Optional CTA
4. Optional website
5. Legal or promotional detail only when supplied

Do not place long paragraphs inside campaign artwork.

## Headline Standard

Preferred length:

- Editorial campaign: 2–6 words
- Social campaign: 2–8 words
- Homepage hero: 3–9 words
- Seasonal campaign: 2–7 words

A strong headline should communicate one dominant idea.

### Discovery

- Discover Something Distinctive
- A New Scent Story

### Sensory

- Freshness, Refined
- Warmth in Every Note
- A Quiet Kind of Bold

### Editorial

- Modern by Nature
- Defined by Detail
- Elegance, Distilled
- The Art of Presence

### Gift

- A Gift to Remember
- Beautifully Chosen
- Give Something Unforgettable
- Wrapped in Possibility

### New Arrival

- Newly Curated
- Just Arrived
- Meet the Latest Edit
- New to Bhalow

These examples are direction, not mandatory copy.

## Supporting Copy

Supporting copy should add information or emotional context rather than repeat the headline.

Preferred length: 6–18 words.

Examples:

- Curated fragrances for every mood, moment, and signature style.
- Discover authentic scents selected for modern fragrance lovers.
- An elevated fragrance edit, chosen with care.

## CTA System

Use clear retail language.

Primary CTAs:

- Shop Now
- Explore the Collection
- Discover More
- Find Your Scent
- View the Edit
- Shop the Collection
- Explore New Arrivals

Use “Learn More” only when the destination is informational.

Avoid:

- Click Here
- Buy It Now!!!
- Hurry Up
- Must Have
- Best Ever
- Guaranteed

## Website Use

Use `www.bhalow.com` only when appropriate to the asset.

- Social campaign: often useful
- Facebook cover: useful when layout permits
- Homepage hero: usually unnecessary inside the visual unless requested
- Website thumbnail: never by default
- Product photography: never by default

## Promotional Copy

Only use price, discount, sale percentage, coupon, shipping promise, stock urgency, or deadline when explicitly supplied.

Do not invent:

- “Limited time”
- “Only a few left”
- “Free shipping”
- “Lowest price”
- “100% authentic” as a formal guarantee
- Savings amounts

When the user supplies a promotion, preserve the exact terms and date.

## Product Claims

Do not state fragrance concentration, longevity, projection, notes, ingredients, gender classification, release year, perfumer, or awards unless verified or provided.

Safe neutral language includes:

- A refined fragrance expression
- A distinctive addition to your collection
- A modern scent for memorable moments
- A curated fragrance discovery

## Luxury Language Control

Use luxury terms selectively.

Preferred:

- Refined
- Curated
- Elegant
- Distinctive
- Modern
- Timeless
- Polished
- Sensory
- Signature
- Elevated

Avoid stacking multiple luxury adjectives in one sentence.

Weak:

> Experience the ultimate luxurious, premium, elegant, sophisticated fragrance masterpiece.

Better:

> Discover a refined fragrance with a distinctive presence.

## Seasonal Copy Logic

### Valentine’s Day

Mood: intimate, warm, romantic, elegant.

Possible direction:

- Scent, Made Personal
- A Beautiful Way to Say It
- Give a Lasting Impression

### Mother’s Day

Mood: thoughtful, graceful, appreciative.

Possible direction:

- Chosen with Love
- A Beautiful Thank You
- Celebrate Her Signature

### Father’s Day

Mood: confident, polished, considered.

Possible direction:

- A Signature Worth Giving
- Chosen for His Style
- Refined Gifts for Him

### Holiday

Mood: celebratory, warm, gift-led, premium.

Possible direction:

- The Fragrance Gift Edit
- Give Something Memorable
- A Season of Beautiful Choices

### Black Friday

Mood: high-contrast retail urgency with luxury restraint.

Use only supplied offer details. Avoid turning the brand into a discount marketplace.

## Platform Copy Rules

### Homepage Hero

- One headline
- One supporting line
- One CTA
- Keep copy block compact
- Do not include navigation or website UI

### Social Poster

- One dominant message
- Mobile-readable
- Optional CTA and website
- Avoid more than three text levels

### Facebook Cover

- Keep critical text inside the mobile-safe center
- Use short brand or campaign statement
- Website may appear near the official logo

### Website Thumbnail

- No copy

### Product Photography

- No copy unless explicitly requested

## Social Caption Structure

When asked for a social-media caption:

1. Opening line or hook
2. One concise product or campaign paragraph
3. Clear CTA
4. Website only when useful
5. Focused hashtags

Do not repeat poster text word-for-word.

## Hashtag Standard

Use a compact set, typically 5–10 relevant hashtags.

Default brand/category pool:

- #Bhalow
- #Fragrance
- #Perfume
- #FragranceLovers
- #LuxuryFragrance
- #ScentOfTheDay
- #PerfumeCollection
- #BeautyFinds

Add product or occasion hashtags only when accurate.

## Copy QA Gate

Confirm:

- Natural US English
- One clear message
- Correct spelling and punctuation
- No unsupported facts
- No invented promotion
- No excessive adjective stacking
- CTA matches the destination
- Copy length fits the platform
- Text does not overpower the product

## Final Copy Rule

Say less, mean more, and never trade trust for hype.
