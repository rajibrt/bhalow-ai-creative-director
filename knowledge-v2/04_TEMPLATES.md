# 04 — Campaign Systems and Production Templates

## Purpose

Provide campaign blueprints that combine creative intent, composition, lighting, camera, copy, and quality constraints.

## 1. Luxury Beauty Campaign

### Use When

- Model + fragrance
- Beauty portrait
- Human-product interaction
- Fashion-led fragrance campaign

### Composition

- Portrait or close beauty crop.
- Face is dominant but product remains clearly readable.
- Product may be held near face, neck, shoulder, or chest.
- Leave controlled space for optional headline.

### Lighting

- Cinematic beauty key light.
- Soft skin highlights.
- Warm or cool rim light based on fragrance mood.
- Background bokeh or controlled falloff.

### Camera

- 50mm to 85mm beauty portrait language.
- Eye-level or slightly lower angle.
- Shallow depth of field without losing product sharpness.

### Copy

- Minimal emotional headline.
- Optional one-line subheading.
- No long paragraph on the image.

### Prompt Block

```text
Create a premium luxury beauty fragrance campaign featuring [model direction] and the uploaded fragrance product. Preserve the exact product identity, geometry, cap, label, color, and packaging. Use a refined beauty portrait composition with natural hand placement, realistic fingers, elegant makeup, premium skin texture, cinematic commercial lighting, controlled background falloff, and strong product visibility. Do not cover the label. Do not deform the bottle. Leave subtle negative space for optional headline. Agency-grade US luxury beauty advertising, realistic photography, product and face both sharply resolved.
```

## 2. Editorial Product Campaign

### Use When

- Single product
- Magazine-inspired ad
- Minimal product storytelling
- Typography-led luxury composition

### Composition

- Product occupies the main hierarchy.
- Use a hand, pedestal, shadow, or one material cue if helpful.
- Minimal negative-space layout.

### Lighting

- Sculptural directional light.
- Controlled contrast.
- Realistic glass highlights and shadows.

### Camera

- Front three-quarter or slight low-angle hero view.
- 70mm to 100mm product language.

### Copy

- 2–6 word headline.
- One short support line only when needed.

### Prompt Block

```text
Create a premium editorial product campaign for the uploaded fragrance. Use the product as the dominant hero with accurate bottle geometry, label, cap, glass, liquid color, and branding. Compose a restrained magazine-style layout with controlled negative space, one elegant supporting material or hand interaction, sculptural lighting, realistic reflections, and minimal editorial typography placement. Avoid clutter, fake branding, busy props, and excessive effects. High-end fragrance magazine advertising, commercial realism, refined and timeless.
```

## 3. Commercial Homepage Hero Visual

### Use When

- Homepage hero
- Landing-page hero
- Collection hero
- Wide commercial banner

### Mandatory Rule

This is a standalone hero visual, not a full website mockup.

Never include navigation, search, account, cart, header, footer, browser chrome, or a complete website interface unless explicitly requested.

### Composition

- Wide 16:9 or requested size.
- Product occupies 35–55% of the width.
- Product on one side, copy zone on the other.
- Include CTA-safe and logo-safe space.

### Prompt Block

```text
Create a standalone premium homepage hero visual for Bhalow, a US-first luxury fragrance e-commerce brand. Use the uploaded product as the main hero and preserve its identity, proportions, cap, label, packaging, and colors. Build a wide commercial composition with the product occupying approximately 35–55% of the frame, generous negative space for headline and subheading, a clear CTA-safe area, and a separate logo-safe area. Do not generate navigation, search, cart, account icons, header, footer, browser frame, or complete website UI. Premium editorial lighting, realistic materials, elegant background, responsive-safe composition, production-ready luxury e-commerce advertising.
```

## 4. Premium Product Photography

### Use When

- Clean product shot
- Studio product photography
- Product detail image
- Commercial packshot

### Prompt Block

```text
Create premium commercial product photography of the uploaded fragrance. Keep the bottle centered or compositionally balanced, fully visible, accurately proportioned, and faithful to the reference. Preserve label, cap, bottle shape, packaging, glass, and liquid color. Use professional softbox lighting, realistic reflections, clean shadow, sharp edges, and a controlled studio background. No marketing copy, no logo recreation, no decorative clutter. High-resolution e-commerce and advertising quality.
```

## 5. Luxury Social Campaign

### Use When

- Facebook or Instagram post
- Product launch
- New arrival
- Promotional campaign

### Composition

- Product-first.
- 1:1 or 4:5 by default.
- Headline zone, optional support copy, CTA, website.
- Mobile-readable.

### Prompt Block

```text
Create a premium social campaign for Bhalow using the uploaded fragrance as the hero product. Preserve the exact product identity. Select art direction based on the fragrance mood and packaging, using restrained luxury materials and controlled decorative elements. Build a mobile-readable composition with a concise headline zone, optional supporting line, CTA area, and clean brand-safe spacing. Do not cover the label, overcrowd the design, or imitate the Bhalow logo. Agency-grade US luxury fragrance advertising.
```

## 6. Luxury CGI Campaign

### Use When

- Cinematic CGI
- Concept-driven product world
- Surreal material environment
- Ingredient-inspired campaign

### Prompt Block

```text
Create a cinematic luxury CGI campaign for the uploaded fragrance. Preserve the exact bottle geometry, label, cap, packaging, and colors. Build a concept-driven environment based on the fragrance family using physically believable glass, stone, water, silk, metal, botanicals, light, or mist. Keep the product as the clearest hero. Use cinematic camera language, refined lighting, realistic reflections and shadows, and controlled effects. Do not hide the label, deform the product, or let the environment overpower it.
```

## Quality Gate for All Campaigns

- Correct campaign category selected
- Product identity preserved
- Product label visible
- Human anatomy correct when applicable
- Platform-safe composition
- No unauthorized logo recreation
- No unsupported claims
- Luxury restraint maintained
- Final prompt is clear and executable
