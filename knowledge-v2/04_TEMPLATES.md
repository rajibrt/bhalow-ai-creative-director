# 04 — Campaign Systems and Production Templates

**Knowledge version:** 3.0.0  
**Status:** Production

## Purpose

Provide adaptable production blueprints. Select the campaign system from the product, audience, platform, and objective; never apply a template mechanically.

## 1. Women's Luxury Campaign

### Use When
Feminine beauty, floral, couture, radiant, romantic, elegant, or fashion-led positioning is appropriate.

### Direction
- Refined portrait or upper-body crop
- Realistic skin and elegant grooming
- Graceful but natural product interaction
- Product 20–40% and label readable
- 50–85mm portrait language
- Beauty key light with controlled rim or background falloff

### Prompt Block
```text
Create a premium women's luxury fragrance campaign using the uploaded product as the verified reference. Preserve the exact bottle, cap, label, packaging, colors, glass, and liquid. Use a refined contemporary beauty or fashion portrait with realistic skin, elegant styling, natural anatomy, believable hands, cinematic commercial lighting, and clear product visibility. Keep the label unobstructed. Select the palette from the product and fragrance positioning rather than defaulting to pink, flowers, or glitter. Agency-grade US luxury fragrance advertising.
```

## 2. Men's Luxury Campaign

### Use When
Masculine, grooming, woody, leather, aromatic, spicy, aquatic, evening, tailored, or confident positioning is appropriate.

### Direction
- Confident portrait or grooming ritual
- Clean premium grooming and wardrobe
- Product near jaw, chest, lapel, wrist, or foreground
- Sculptural key light and readable shadows
- 50–85mm portrait language
- Avoid aggression, excessive smoke, bodybuilder posing, and generic black-and-gold clichés

### Prompt Block
```text
Create a premium men's luxury fragrance campaign using the uploaded product as the verified reference. Preserve the exact bottle, cap, label, packaging, colors, glass, and liquid. Feature a polished adult male model with confident natural posture, premium grooming, elevated wardrobe, realistic skin, anatomically correct hands, and believable product interaction. Use sculptural cinematic lighting, controlled contrast, strong product separation, and clear label visibility. Avoid aggressive posing, excessive smoke, nightclub clichés, or generic hyper-masculine styling. Agency-grade US luxury grooming and fragrance advertising.
```

## 3. Unisex Luxury Campaign

### Use When
The fragrance is niche, modern, artistic, minimalist, shared, or gender-neutral.

### Prompt Block
```text
Create a premium unisex luxury fragrance campaign for the uploaded product. Preserve the exact product identity. Use contemporary gender-neutral fashion, refined grooming, architectural or minimalist composition, restrained materials, balanced silhouettes, and concept-led editorial lighting. Keep the product clearly dominant and avoid forcing masculine or feminine stereotypes. High-end US niche-fragrance advertising.
```

## 4. Couple or Shared-Fragrance Campaign

### Use When
The concept involves gifting, intimacy, shared fragrance, celebration, or paired products.

### Prompt Block
```text
Create a premium couple or shared-fragrance campaign using the uploaded product reference. Preserve every product exactly. Use believable adult interaction, natural body language, elegant wardrobe, realistic hands, one clear focal hierarchy, and refined cinematic lighting. Keep all labels readable and avoid stereotyped romance, wedding styling, or awkward product passing unless explicitly requested.
```

## 5. Editorial Product Campaign

### Direction
- One dominant product
- Product 55–80% of hierarchy
- One supporting material family
- Sculptural directional light
- Front three-quarter or subtle low-angle view
- 2–6 word headline when copy is requested

### Prompt Block
```text
Create a premium editorial product campaign for the uploaded fragrance. Preserve accurate bottle geometry, label, cap, glass, liquid color, packaging, and branding. Use a restrained magazine-style layout, controlled negative space, one elegant material cue, sculptural lighting, realistic reflections, and minimal editorial typography placement. Avoid clutter, fake branding, and excessive effects.
```

## 6. Commercial Homepage Hero

### Mandatory Rule
This is a standalone hero visual, not a full website mockup.

### Direction
- Wide requested dimensions
- Product occupies 35–55% of width
- Product on one side; copy zone on the other
- CTA-safe and logo-safe space
- Responsive-safe composition
- No navigation, search, account, cart, header, footer, browser chrome, or full website UI

### Prompt Block
```text
Create a standalone premium homepage hero visual for Bhalow using the uploaded product as the main hero. Preserve product identity and proportions. Build a wide commercial composition with the product occupying approximately 35–55% of the frame, generous negative space for headline and support copy, a CTA-safe area, and a separate logo-safe area. Do not generate navigation, search, cart, account icons, header, footer, browser frame, or complete website UI. Premium editorial lighting, realistic materials, responsive-safe luxury e-commerce composition.
```

## 7. Premium Product Photography

```text
Create premium commercial product photography of the uploaded fragrance. Keep the product fully visible, accurately proportioned, and faithful to the reference. Preserve label, cap, bottle shape, packaging, glass, and liquid color. Use professional softbox lighting, realistic reflections, clean shadow, sharp edges, and a controlled studio background. No marketing copy, logo recreation, or decorative clutter.
```

## 8. Luxury Social Campaign

### Direction
- 1:1 or 4:5 unless another ratio is requested
- Product-first hierarchy
- Mobile-readable headline zone
- Optional support copy, CTA, and website
- Art direction selected from product and audience

```text
Create a premium social campaign for Bhalow using the uploaded fragrance as the hero product. Preserve the exact product identity. Select women's, men's, unisex, editorial, CGI, lifestyle, or grooming direction from the product and objective. Build a mobile-readable composition with concise copy-safe areas and premium restraint. Do not cover the label, imitate the Bhalow logo, or invent product facts, prices, discounts, warranties, or claims.
```

## 9. Luxury CGI Campaign

```text
Create a cinematic luxury CGI campaign for the uploaded fragrance. Preserve the exact bottle geometry, label, cap, packaging, and colors. Build one coherent concept-driven environment using physically believable glass, stone, water, silk, metal, botanicals, light, resin, or mist. Keep the product as the clearest hero. Effects must not hide, deform, or overpower the product.
```

## 10. Website Thumbnail

```text
Create a clean 1:1 e-commerce website thumbnail from the uploaded product reference. Show the product only on a white or soft neutral background with safe margins and an optional realistic drop shadow. Preserve exact geometry, label, cap, packaging, colors, and relative scale. No copy, CTA, logo, campaign props, decorative background, or invented product elements.
```

## Quality Gate

- Correct operating mode and campaign selected
- Correct audience direction without stereotypes
- Product identity and label preserved
- Anatomy and hands correct when applicable
- Platform-safe composition
- No unauthorized logo recreation
- No invented prices, discounts, warranties, specifications, ingredients, certifications, authenticity guarantees, or claims
- Luxury restraint maintained
- Final output is clear, executable, and commercially usable
